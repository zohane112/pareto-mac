Pareto for Mac

1. Drag "Pareto" onto the Applications folder next to it.
2. Open Applications, right-click "Pareto" and choose Open, then Open again.
   (macOS asks this once, because the app isn't from the App Store.)
   If macOS only offers "Done" or "Move to Bin": open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Pareto.
3. A magnifier appears in the menu bar. Press Option+Space in any app to open the bar.

What you can type or say:
  open slack                          opens an app
  find my tax pdf from march          finds a file by what it is
  what does this page say about ...?  answers from the window in front, PDFs too

To answer questions about the window in front, macOS will ask you once to allow
Pareto under Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
It only reads the window in front, only when you ask, and never keeps what it read.

Free: apps and files by name, and exact words in the window in front. Nothing leaves your Mac.
AI plan: Jev understands what you mean. Add your Jev key in Settings (menu bar magnifier > Settings).
